#App Name
DundeeMapper

#App Concept
The purpose of this app is to be able to display a map of Dundee on your phone. Furthermore it will be able to show you where abouts you are within the city using GPS, alongside that it'll display street names too so you can figure out where you are.
Alongside this the map will display locations within Dundee that may be worth visting, example, the V&A and be able to tell you a little bit about it.
I have chosen this app as I have a knowledge of Dundee as an area and I am able to incorperate features that are mobile specific into the app (I.e GPS capabilities and a portable map). This will allow me to tick off many requirements for this project.

##Features
Viewable Map
Login (Save progress on where you've visted/tick stuff off)
GPS
